# Community AI Lab

We're starting a community project to build AI models together.

People contribute code, research, data, testing or GPU time. We want to record that work fairly and explore how contributors could share in the revenue from commercially hosted models. **The platform and the revenue-sharing agreement still need to be built.**

## What we're working on

### [Contributor Platform](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/platform)
A simple way to find tasks, contribute to AI projects, have work reviewed and keep a record of contributions. We're also exploring a credit system and paid inference through hosted model services.

[View tasks](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/platform/issues) · [Roadmap](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/platform/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)

### [Speech-to-Speech Model](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/speech-to-speech)
Our first model project: an AI system that listens and responds in speech. The goal is to make it useful locally and through a hosted API. We'll start by testing existing approaches and build from there.

[View tasks](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/speech-to-speech/issues)

## How to contribute

We're looking for software engineers, AI researchers, audio specialists, GPU and distributed-systems developers, and people who can help with testing, documentation or organising the work.

1. **Choose a project** using the links above.
2. Open its **Issues** tab and find something you'd like to help with.
3. Comment on the issue to say you're interested, or suggest a task that's missing.
4. Share your code or findings in a pull request or issue for review.

You don't need to attend a meeting or join a formal team. Pick a task that suits you and get started.

**Contributor terms:** We intend to record accepted contributions and develop an agreement covering future commercial revenue sharing. Those terms are not final and no payment or ownership rights are promised yet. Please don't submit confidential work or data/audio that you don't have permission to share.
