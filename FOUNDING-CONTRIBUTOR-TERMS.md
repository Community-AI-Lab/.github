# Community AI Lab — Founding Contributor Acknowledgement (Test Draft v0.1)

**DRAFT — TEST ONLY. Not a final Contributor Licence Agreement.**

This text is intended to test the GitHub acknowledgement flow while Community AI Lab develops a full contributor licence and revenue-sharing agreement. It does not grant a commercial-use licence and is **not** a promise of royalties, equity or other financial rights. A legally identified contracting party and legal review are required before a binding agreement is offered.

## 1. Purpose

Community AI Lab proposes to build software for collaborative AI research, and its first speech-to-speech model. Contributors may help with code, research, evaluation, authorised datasets and GPU computation.

## 2. Rights to contribute

You confirm that you created, own or are otherwise authorised to submit the work and that you will identify relevant third-party licences and restrictions. Do not submit employer-confidential material, credentials, personal data or voice recordings without appropriate permission and consent.

## 3. Ownership and use

You retain ownership of your original contribution. By submitting a GitHub pull request you allow it to be publicly displayed, inspected and tested for review through GitHub, subject to GitHub's terms. **This draft does not authorise commercial relicensing, model training for sale, paid deployment, or sublicensing of your contribution.** Any additional commercial or open-source licence permissions must be agreed separately before they are relied on.

## 4. Future revenue sharing

The proposal is to record verified contributions and design a credit system so contributors can share in a defined pool of future commercial model revenue. The scoring, distribution formula, duration, payment terms and legal entitlements have not yet been agreed. No cash or ownership rights arise from acknowledging this draft.

## 5. What the GitHub acknowledgement means

For the initial GitHub workflow test, posting the requested acknowledgement confirms **only that you have read this draft**. It is **not** an enforceable final CLA signature or acceptance of commercial-licensing or future royalty terms. You will be asked to review any later agreement separately.

## 6. Next steps

Before a legally operative CLA is enabled, Community AI Lab must identify the contracting party, obtain legal review, publish the approved agreement and decide how changes will be versioned and recorded. Contributions cannot be commercially used just because their authors acknowledged this draft.
